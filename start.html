GETTING STARTED

Your first session with a Micro Tools Studio Complete Kit.

01

Unzip. Open the guide.

Save your download and order details. Extract the ZIP completely and open START_HERE.html. Keep an untouched copy of the package as your master.

Start with the browser workspace.

Open the file ending in _Workspace.html from your extracted kit. It starts blank. Add one record and check the summary. Save a JSON backup before you close. Use Import backup to restore your records later. Browser storage stays on the device and can be cleared.

Each kit includes its own getting-started guide. InvoiceForge handles balances and follow-ups; Cert Pai handles renewal records; Site Intellect handles review findings; Claim Proof handles evidence and a submission index.

02

Choose the file for the job.

Payment tracker · XLSXRecord invoices, settled receipts, balances and next actions.

Complete workbook · PDFRead the worked example and fill in project and invoice records.

Editable templates · TXTAdapt records and follow-up messages in your usual text editor.

Client communication templates · HTMLCopy a payment request, project update or handoff message.

03

Using the optional InvoiceForge spreadsheet?

The spreadsheet contains two clearly marked fictional examples. In your working copy, clear input cells A12:F13 and I12:K13. Keep formula columns G, H and L. Then enter one real invoice and any confirmed payment.

Check the remaining balance before adding more records. The spreadsheet has 100 prepared rows, ending at row 111. Use another copy when it is full.

04

Choose, review, send.

Set a next action and review date. Copy a suitable message, replace all bracketed prompts, check the amount and send through your normal email app. Confirm funds in your own records before marking an invoice paid.

Software and saving tips

Use a desktop or laptop and an app that opens XLSX files. Native Excel, Google Sheets and Numbers compatibility testing is pending. For the fillable PDF, save a test entry, close the file and reopen it to check that your reader keeps form values. Use concise notes; the text templates are better for longer details. Keep regular backups.
