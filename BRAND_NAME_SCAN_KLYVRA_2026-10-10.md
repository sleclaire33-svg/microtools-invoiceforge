# Brand name scan: Klyvra and spelling variants

Scan date: 2026-10-10
Scope: public web search for exact names, close spellings, product/company uses, and discoverable trademark references. This is an initial screening, not legal advice or a complete trademark clearance.

## Executive verdict

**Do not proceed with Klyvra Tools. Move it to the rejected list.**

The exact root **Klyvra** is already actively used by at least three technology/app products:
- Klyvra AI security platform at https://klyvra.ai/ (enterprise AI security, posture scanning, runtime guardrails, and remediation).
- Klyvra sovereign productivity suite at https://klyvra.io/ (self-hosted software suite with multiple productivity applications).
- Klyvra app at https://klyvra.app/ (a surf/wave-condition coaching and session-tracking app).

That is a meaningful existing-product collision. Even if a trademark database search eventually showed no blocking registration for a specific country/class, using the same distinctive root for a software/tool brand risks confusion and creates avoidable domain/search/handle problems. Adding “Tools” does not remove the shared root. Do not spend on a domain, logo, or campaign around this name.

## Copyright vs. trademark

The main concern here is **trademark/brand confusion**, not copyright. Copyright generally does not protect a short brand name by itself. Trademark rights and unfair-competition/passing-off rules vary by country, product category, and market reach; domain availability is a separate question. A name can be risky even when an exact trademark registration is not immediately obvious in a search engine.

## Recommended next move

Do not keep tweaking Klyvra by one letter. Several natural variants are already used, and one near-variant (Klyvexa) is in AI automation/tools. Start a fresh naming direction with more phonetic distance from Klyvra/Klyvera/Klyvexa/Klivora.

For each new candidate, do:
1. Exact, plural, spaced, phonetic and typo web searches.
2. Direct trademark-register searches in target jurisdictions/classes (software downloads and SaaS/software services).
3. Company-register searches.
4. Registrar check for the exact domain and sensible alternatives.
5. Direct handle checks across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook and X.
6. Review by trademark counsel if the brand will be used commercially across multiple markets.
