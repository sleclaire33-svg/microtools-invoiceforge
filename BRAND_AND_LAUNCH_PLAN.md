# Brand shortlist and launch campaign (working draft)

Last reviewed: 2026-10-10

## Decision status

Keep **MicroTools Hub** as the umbrella concept and **InvoiceForge** as the invoice product until a new umbrella name is approved and cleared. Do not change the public site, buy a domain, or create social accounts based only on a quick web search.

Naming requirements:
- Distinctive 5–7 letter root plus a clear companion word (Tools, Works, Studio, or Labs).
- Easy to pronounce and spell internationally.
- Broad enough for free utilities and future small-business products.
- No obvious direct software/company/trademark conflict in an initial web screen.
- Search, trademark, domain, and social-handle checks are separate; this shortlist is not legal clearance.

## Clearance checklist before final selection

For the final two candidates:
1. Search exact, spaced, phonetic, and similar spellings in relevant trademark databases for the intended markets and classes, especially downloadable software and SaaS/software services.
2. Check company registries and ordinary web/search results for direct competitors.
3. Check exact .com and practical alternative domains with a registrar; do not assume availability from search snippets.
4. Check Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X handles directly.
5. Prefer the candidate with fewer conflicts, a clean domain/handle set, and a clear pronunciation. Consider professional legal clearance before commercial adoption.

## Two-week social launch campaign

Use the approved umbrella brand once cleared. Before then, use InvoiceForge/product-specific material without announcing an unapproved parent brand. Start with one primary channel (short-form video) and repurpose to LinkedIn; do not try to maintain every platform daily.

### Week 1 — demonstrate usefulness
- Day 1 — Teaser: “Small-business admin should take minutes, not your afternoon.” Show the InvoiceForge interface; no earnings claims.
- Day 2 — 20-second demo: create an invoice, add line items, preview totals, and print/save as PDF.
- Day 3 — Free tool spotlight: percentage calculator for discounts, fees, and price changes.
- Day 4 — Free tool spotlight: QR generator; demonstrate a QR code for a public website URL.
- Day 5 — Freelancer tip: what to include on an invoice (number, date, line items, payment terms, contact details).
- Day 6 — Product walkthrough: show what is included in the $14.99 Template Pack; state exactly what buyers receive.
- Day 7 — Feedback post: ask freelancers which admin task wastes the most time; collect replies and improve the tool.

### Week 2 — launch and learn
- Day 8 — Launch post: free utilities and InvoiceForge, with a clear link and honest feature list.
- Day 9 — 15-second invoice workflow demo: draft → review → print/save.
- Day 10 — Educational post: a polite payment-reminder sequence; point to the relevant Template Pack content.
- Day 11 — Lifetime Pro walkthrough: show the invoice workflow, QA checklist, quote-to-cash tracker, scope/onboarding/handoff resources. Price: $29.
- Day 12 — FAQ: explain free tools versus paid downloadable resources, checkout, delivery-link expiry, and support.
- Day 13 — Build-in-public post: one improvement made from user feedback; do not claim customer numbers without evidence.
- Day 14 — Recap: best free tools, paid options, request one specific piece of feedback.

## Measurement and low-maintenance rules

Track only a few useful signals at first: visits, free-tool usage, checkout starts, completed purchases, refund/support requests, and which post links drive visits. Do not add paid analytics until the checkout path is proven. Use tagged links and a simple weekly spreadsheet; avoid collecting personal data that is not needed.

Do not promise passive income, guaranteed results, customer counts, or unsupported payment methods. Do not announce paid launch until sandbox purchases for both products, signed download delivery, cancellation/error handling, and webhook verification pass. Live payments require a deliberate owner decision and a small real transaction test.
