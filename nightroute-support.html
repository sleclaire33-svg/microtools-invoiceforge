NightRoute privacy & support

Last updated 7 November 2026 for NightRoute 1.0. App Store release is in preparation.

Your mixes stay with you

NightRoute generates its sounds on your device. It does not require an account or internet access for playback. The app includes no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics and does not send your settings, saved mix names or app activity to Micro Tools Studio.

Local settings and backups

Your mixer settings and saved mixes are stored locally on your device. When you export a backup, you choose where the file is saved. If you choose iCloud Drive or another cloud provider in Files, that provider handles the file under its own policies. Mix names are included in backups, so avoid putting sensitive information in them. Keep backups only where you intend to share or store them.

You can remove saved mixes in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data; any backups you exported remain in the locations you chose until you delete them there. Device and cloud backups are governed by your Apple and storage-provider settings.

Purchases and diagnostics

Apple handles App Store purchases and payment details. Micro Tools Studio does not receive your card details through NightRoute. Apple may process diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple's policies.

Support

Email sean@microtoolsstudio.com for help. Include the app version, your device model and what happened. Please do not include passwords or payment details. We receive your email address and any information you choose to send and use it to respond to your request. Ask us by email if you want support correspondence deleted; records required for legal or transaction purposes may need to be retained.

Common questions

Why is there no sound? Turn up at least one mixer layer, check device volume and your selected audio output, then tap Play. Calls and disconnected headphones pause playback; resume it when ready.

How does the timer work? It begins with playback. Pause freezes it. Changing the duration or loading a mix restarts it. Choose Until stopped for an unlimited session.

How do I move my mixes? Choose Export mix backup. On the other device, use Restore a mix backup and confirm replacement. Export the existing mixes first if you want to keep both sets.

Are these recordings? No. Rain texture, carriage hum and room fan are original synthesized sounds generated on your device.

Changes

We will update this page when NightRoute's data practices change. The date above identifies this version.
