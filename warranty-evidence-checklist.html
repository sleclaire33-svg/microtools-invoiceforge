PRACTICAL WORKFLOW / CLAIMPROOF

Keep warranty evidence easy to find.

For households and small businesses organizing purchases and warranty follow-ups.

01 / CHECK

Record the purchase

Keep the item, seller, purchase date, order reference and warranty source together. Verify coverage and deadlines against the seller or manufacturer’s terms.

02 / CHECK

Keep original evidence

Store the original receipt, photos and correspondence in a secure folder. Record their filenames or references in the kit; the workspace does not embed attachments.

03 / CHECK

Build a factual timeline

Record when the issue appeared, what happened and whom you contacted. Separate your observations from conclusions you have not verified.

04 / CHECK

Review before sending

Choose relevant evidence, check the attachment index and send the actual files through the seller’s accepted channel. Keep a copy and set your next follow-up date.
