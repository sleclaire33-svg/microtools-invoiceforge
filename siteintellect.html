Is this an AI website auditor?

No. SiteIntellect is a structured workspace for reviews you perform. It does not crawl a website, scan for vulnerabilities or automatically find errors.

Why use it alongside my existing issue tracker?

It offers a small, portable review record with reproduction details, retest evidence and a plain-text handoff. If your existing system already handles that well, you may not need this kit.

Does a completed review certify accessibility or security?

No. A completed finding records your own review and retest. It is not a certification, security assessment or guarantee of compliance.

What can I give a client?

You can share reports and records created for your own client work. The blank kit and source templates are for your business’s use and cannot be resold or redistributed as a competing product.
