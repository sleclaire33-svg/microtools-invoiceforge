PRACTICAL WORKFLOW / SITEINTELLECT

Turn vague website feedback into a testable fix.

For freelance website builders and small service teams reviewing their own sites.

01 / CHECK

Describe what you did

Record the page, device/browser and exact steps. Use fictional or non-sensitive examples when sharing a report.

02 / CHECK

Separate expected and actual behavior

Example: “Selecting the pricing button should open the product checkout; it returns to the home page.” This is more useful than “the button is broken.”

03 / CHECK

Prioritize the customer impact

A blocked purchase or inaccessible core action deserves attention before a small decorative issue. Assign an owner and a clear next step.

04 / CHECK

Retest the same steps

After the change, repeat the original steps and record the observed result and evidence. Keep the issue open if the result cannot be confirmed.

Try the workflow with a fictional example.

SiteIntellect organizes manual review findings and retests. It does not automatically scan a website or certify accessibility, security or legal compliance.
