Who is this kit most useful for?

An office or training coordinator who maintains a manageable set of renewal records and wants one clear next review for each. It suits a manual review routine; it does not replace a regulated credential-management system.

Will it remind people automatically?

No. Date bands and next-action records help you review the list yourself. Use your own calendar or messaging service for notifications.

Does it verify a certificate or determine compliance?

No. You must check the original issuing source. The tool records your review; it does not confirm qualifications or make compliance decisions.

Can I work without a spreadsheet app?

Yes. Extract the ZIP, open START_HERE.html and launch CertPai_Workspace.html in a current desktop browser. The XLSX and fillable PDF are optional, separate companions. They do not synchronize with the workspace.
