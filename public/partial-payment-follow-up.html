PRACTICAL WORKFLOW / INVOICEFORGE

A client paid part of an invoice. What next?

For freelance designers, writers and consultants managing staged payments.

01 / CHECK

Confirm the money received

Check settled receipts in your payment provider or bank records. A client saying they paid is a useful lead, but it is not a confirmed receipt.

02 / CHECK

Recalculate the outstanding balance

Fictional example: $120 invoiced minus a confirmed $50 payment leaves $70. A second confirmed $50 payment leaves $20. Keep the currency and invoice reference with the record.

03 / CHECK

Write a precise follow-up

Example: “Thanks for your payment toward invoice IF-104. My records show $100 received against the $120 invoice, leaving $20 outstanding. Could you confirm the expected payment date?” Check the facts and your agreed terms before sending.

04 / CHECK

Choose the next review date

Record the message and a sensible review date. Update the balance when another payment settles. Save a backup after changing records.

Try the workflow with a fictional example.

InvoiceForge helps you keep the balance and next action together. It does not collect money, verify bank receipts or send messages automatically.
