How is this different from InvoiceForge?

InvoiceForge focuses on invoices, remaining balances and payment follow-ups. Agency OS focuses on readiness before kickoff, working arrangements and changes to agreed scope. Choose by the job you need to do; the kits do not share data automatically.

Does it confirm payments or enforce a contract?

No. You check settled funds in your own payment records and confirm approvals against your agreement. Readiness results are prompts for the responsible person to review the underlying evidence.

Can the whole team edit it together?

The browser workspace is local to one browser. The spreadsheet can be imported into your own Google Sheets account and shared under your own permissions. Be careful with edit access to formula cells.

Is this the original $750 Agency OS service?

This is the revised self-service workflow kit. It does not include a custom setup or implementation service. If you already bought a different offer, keep your original order details and contact support to confirm what your purchase includes.
