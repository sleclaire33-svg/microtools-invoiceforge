Privacy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Micro Tools Studio provides browser-based tools and digital products. We aim to collect and retain as little information as practical.

Information you enter

Invoice details entered into the invoice builder are processed in your browser to generate the preview and print/save workflow. The payment follow-up tool stores the client name, invoice number, amount, currency, due date, status, sender name and optional context in your browser's local storage on that device. This follow-up data is not sent to our server by that tool. Anyone with access to your browser profile may be able to view it, and clearing browser data may delete it. Export a backup if you need to keep it. Do not enter sensitive personal or payment-card information. We do not need an account for these basic tools.

Downloaded workspaces and checkout recovery

The downloadable browser workspaces store your working records locally in your browser and export files to your device. They do not call external services. Keep exported backups secure; browser storage may be cleared. Checkout stores the pending order and verified download link in session storage in your current browser tab to support payment recovery. Saving order details downloads a text file to your device. The support form prepares an email in your email application and does not submit it automatically.

Payments

Current kit purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy and its payment providers. Checkout collects the information needed to process your order and deliver files. We can view purchase and customer details in the merchant dashboard for support. We do not receive your full payment-card number or payment-account password. Older direct PayPal purchases remain subject to that provider’s data practices.

QR generation

When you use the QR Generator, the text or URL you enter is sent to the QR-code image service used by the site to generate the image. Do not submit confidential information to the QR Generator.

Website visit measurement

We use Metricool to count visits to selected public storefront and preview pages. Our integration sends the public page address without query strings or fragments and, when available, only the referring website’s origin. Metricool receives technical connection data, including your IP address, which it uses for visit and country reporting. The integration does not use analytics cookies, send form contents, or run inside downloaded workspaces. Checkout, payment confirmation and browser work-tool pages are excluded. We honor supported Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals. Visits may be missing when tracking is blocked; these counts do not identify purchases or establish sales attribution.

Hosting and technical data

The current website uses Vercel hosting. Historical deployment resources may remain on Netlify. Like most web services, hosting and security systems may process technical information such as IP address, browser information, request timestamps, and error information for delivery, security, and reliability.

Digital purchases

Lemon Squeezy stores current kit orders and provides receipt and file-access links. For older direct PayPal purchases, our server receives the order and capture information needed to verify payment and provide the purchased content. Signed download links may contain a product identifier and expiration information.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email sean@microtoolsstudio.com.

External services

When you use the QR generator, the text or URL entered is sent to the external QR image service described above. Lemon Squeezy, payment providers and hosting providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the service, payment methods, hosting, or data practices change.
