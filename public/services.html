BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

A clear next step.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We review the task, source volume and availability, then agree a written scope, delivery date, final price, applicable tax and cancellation/refund terms before invoicing. Preparing the form does not send an email or place an order.

What should I send first?

A short description of the result you need, the approximate record or page count, available file formats and your deadline. Tell us who can approve the facts. Describe confidential material without attaching it; source files are shared only through an agreed route after the handling terms are confirmed.

Will AI be used with my files?

We agree the permitted tools, information and collaborators before processing project files. Sending an enquiry does not give blanket permission for AI use. If your project requires manual-only handling, include that in your request so the scope and price can be assessed. Read how service enquiries and files are handled.

How are corrections and final approval handled?

Your written scope sets the deliverables, revision allowance and acceptance criteria. We retain source references, flag missing facts and ask the named approver to review the final version. Course-document work also depends on a qualified reviewer, an agreed quote and testing of the exact final files.

Do I need to commit to monthly work?

No. Start with an agreed one-time project. Monthly upkeep is optional, separately agreed and useful only when there is recurring work to maintain.
