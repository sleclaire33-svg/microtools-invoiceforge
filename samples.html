TEMPLATE PACK SAMPLE 3 OF 3

Monthly admin checklist

Earlier writing sample · Adapt before using

  • Match sent invoices to payment records.
  • Follow up on overdue balances professionally.
  • Save receipts and categorize business expenses.
  • Review upcoming renewals, subscriptions, and tax dates.
  • Back up invoices, agreements, and project approvals.
  • Review unpaid work and upcoming cash commitments.
  • Confirm tax/accounting treatment with a qualified local professional when needed.

WORKFLOW CHECKLIST SAMPLE

Invoice quality-control checklist

Earlier checklist sample · Adapt before using

  • Correct business and client details.
  • Unique invoice number and issue date.
  • Clear line-item descriptions, quantities, and rates.
  • Correct arithmetic, tax treatment, discount, and currency.
  • Due date and agreed payment instructions.
  • Relevant purchase-order or project reference.
  • Terms consistent with the signed agreement and local law.
  • PDF opens and is readable on desktop and mobile.
  • Copy saved securely and tracker updated.
